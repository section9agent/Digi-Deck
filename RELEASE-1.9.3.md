# Digi-Deck 1.9.3

> **This build does not include the pre-made microcassette database.** The installer has no database page and the app never imports one. Your own library is not touched.

## Highlights

### Library animations
Press **Play** in the library and the album comes out of its packaging and goes onto the player. **Clear** the playlist after **Stop** (for albums in your library) and it goes back and returns to the Library. Click anywhere or press **Esc** to skip one. You can turn them off in **Settings → General**.

- **Vinyl**: the jacket comes forward and the record slides out (12" from the right, 7" from the top), then is placed on the turntable. 12" jackets are 4 mm thick; 7" sleeves are paper.
- **CD**: the jewel case (10.2 mm) opens on its hinge, with the booklet on the left and the tray on the right. The disc (1.2 mm) is lifted off the hub and set in the player. Putting it back lowers it onto the hub and closes the lid. **3" CDs** slide out of a sleeve.
- **Cassette**: the case comes forward upright, showing its J-card, then turns over to the back (tape and back flap) and onto its side. The clear pocket tips open; the back flap and the tape stay put, and the tape slides out from under the flap. The J-card wraps the case: cover on the front, spine on the hinge edge, back flap on the back (a short flap, or a full-back J-card).
- **Microcassette**: works the same way as the cassette, with the microcassette case. The deck takes the tape in through its own compartment door.
- **Reel to reel**: the 7.5" box lid has flaps over the top, right and bottom, and opens to the left. The reel is lifted out and set on the left spindle, then threaded. Putting it away winds up the slack, takes the tape off the guides and lowers the reel back into its box.

### New look
- Brushed-metal and blue interface; the toolbar, transport and dialog buttons are raised metal keys that press down when clicked.
- **CD player face-lift** in the style of a late-90s flat portable: gloss-black top, brushed-silver control strip and a half-moon key cluster (Prev / Vol − / Vol + / Next around Play/Pause). The LCD is unchanged. A new **EJECT** key puts in the next disc and only appears on multi-disc albums.

### Vinyl
- More realistic grooves: a glossy edge bead, then a **lead-in groove** that spirals in to the first song (the needle now drops there). Songs have a fine groove texture with light and dark rings, and a glossy band separates songs. The **lead-out** spirals in to a **locked groove**, followed by dead wax with an etched matrix number.
- Fixed: the spindle no longer shows through a record while it flips.

### Reel to reel
- Pick a **reel colour**, including the new **tan**. The colour also shows under see-through parts of a full reel label.
- **Full reel labels** are supported. Normal labels are cropped to the label shape, and the side-B label is left off when that slot is empty.
- Reels show their thickness when they are turned over: two flanges, hollow down to the hub.

### Editing & artwork
- **Image adjuster**: nudge buttons for zoom and a new **rotate** control. Labels open zoomed and centred on the part that will be shown.
- **CD**: the library preview uses the new jewel-case art. The tray card wraps the shelf spine again, and the adjuster shows the spine | back | spine layout.
- Using a **release URL / ID** fills empty or generic image slots only; artwork you set yourself is kept.
- **Colours**: black is now properly black, and **tan** is new for cassettes and reels.

### Fixes
- Microcassette: removed the square rim around the shell, and the box that showed around the tape in the case animation.
- Cassette and microcassette: the tape no longer stays visible in the deck while it is being carried away.
- Reel colour now shows on reels with a full reel label.
