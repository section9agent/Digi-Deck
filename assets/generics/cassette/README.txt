Generic cassette art for tapes that have no album information.
Put the two folders here (or point Settings > genericsDir at them):
  jcards/   one is picked at random per tape and saved in the database as its J-card
  labels/   one is picked at random per side label (the built-in generic label is one of the options)
The app also looks in: C:\Users\secti\Desktop\digi-deck MediaPlayer\dont delete\Cassette Generics
